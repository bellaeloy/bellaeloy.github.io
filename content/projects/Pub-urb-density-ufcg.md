## Description

Co-authored with PhD. Mauro Normando Macêdo Barros Filho, the article “Urban Density and Parametric Urbanism: Computational Code for the Simulation of Urban Parameters” was published in the Revista de Morfologia Urbana in 2020. The research investigates the use of computational tools and parametric modeling to support the analysis and visualization of urban regulations related to land use and density. By developing an algorithm using Rhinoceros3D and Grasshopper, the study proposes a computational system capable of simulating urban parameters and generating scenarios to evaluate their impacts on urban form and occupation patterns. The work highlights the potential of digital technologies to enhance decision-making processes in urban planning by enabling more informed, experimental, and data-driven approaches to sustainable and resilient city design.
