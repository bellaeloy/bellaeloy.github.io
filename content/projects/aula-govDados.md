### Description

Organized and facilitated a three-session study group for researchers at Nomads.usp focused on **data governance**, **information modeling**, and **collective approaches to urban data**. The program combined guided discussions of key literature—including *All Data Are Local* (Loukissas, 2019)—with collaborative activities to develop and critically evaluate prototypes for a shared information modeling platform. As organizer and facilitator, I designed the program, selected the readings, coordinated the meetings, and led discussions on data governance frameworks, values embedded in information systems, and their implications for equitable urban public policymaking.
