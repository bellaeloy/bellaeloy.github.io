The riverbank floods every year. The city fenced it off. People still cross it,
because the fence adds 900 m to the walk to the market.

## The approach

The design accepts the flood. It splits the bank into three bands:

1. **Wet band.** Reed beds and a gravel path. The band floods, and it recovers.
2. **Middle band.** A raised timber deck on piles. It stays dry in a normal
   flood year.
3. **Dry band.** The market extension, a play area and the bus stop.

## Materials

The deck uses reclaimed hardwood from a demolished warehouse on the same street.
The gravel comes from the dredge spoil of the river.

## Status

The design won a municipal competition. Construction of the first 300 m starts
in 2026.
