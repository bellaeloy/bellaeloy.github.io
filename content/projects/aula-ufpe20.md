### Description

I collaborated in the graduate course Advanced Topics in Architecture and Urbanism: Generative Design Systems at the Federal University of Pernambuco (UFPE), where I developed and delivered a 15-hour short course titled **"City Pattern: Parametric Code for the Computational Translation of Christopher Alexander’s Patterns Related to Urban Form and Life"**. The workshop introduced a parametric methodology for translating Christopher Alexander’s pattern language into computational design workflows, demonstrating how generative design tools can support the analysis and creation of more human-centered urban environments.
