### Description

Invited by the Graduate Program in Urban Development at the Federal University of Pernambuco (UFPE), I delivered the workshop **"Parametric Modeling in Architecture and Urbanism"** as part of the course Special Topics in 2D Geometry. The workshop introduced participants to the principles and applications of parametric modeling in architectural and urban design, demonstrating how computational design methods can support more flexible, data-driven, and innovative design processes.
