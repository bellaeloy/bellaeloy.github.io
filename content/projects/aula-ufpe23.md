### Description

As part of the **BIM – Digital Design and Fabrication in the Built Environment** postgraduate specialization program at the Federal University of Pernambuco (UFPE), I designed and delivered the workshop **"Parametric Facade"**. Offered within the course *Rapid Prototyping and Digital Fabrication: From Theory to Practice*, the workshop introduced participants to parametric approaches for facade design, exploring computational modeling techniques and digital fabrication workflows for the built environment.
