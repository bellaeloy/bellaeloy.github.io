## Description

Co-authored with Marcelo Tramontano, the paper “Horizontal Data Governance in Urban Space: A Systemic Proposal for Collaborative Integration Among Social Agents” was presented at the XVII International Seminar on Urbanism Research (SIIU 2025), held at the Pontifical Catholic University of Campinas (PUC-Campinas). Our research proposes a systemic framework for fostering collaboration among public institutions, civil society organizations, and social groups through shared data governance and collaborative information modeling. Drawing on Decolonial Thinking, General Systems Theory, Cybernetics, and interdisciplinary perspectives from social sciences and computing, the study examines how data production and algorithmic systems shape urban space, often reinforcing inequalities and excluding vulnerable populations. The paper explores alternative models for communication, virtual communities, and collective knowledge production, contributing to more inclusive and democratic approaches to urban governance in the Global South.
