## Description

Urban development plan for the Itaipuaçu neighborhood in Maricá, Rio de Janeiro, integrating multiple scales of analysis, from the local community and municipality to the metropolitan, regional, national, and global contexts linked to pre-salt oil exploration. The project proposed a multifunctional network connecting environmental areas, residents, and public services. It developed urban planning instruments to support a public land bank and emphasized resilience to extreme events, including sea-level rise and flooding, while promoting adaptation of the urban fabric to climate change. The initiative involved the Municipality of Maricá, CODEMAR, TCRE Engenharia Ltda., and local residents through public consultations.

## My role

I was responsible for producing maps, indicators, and analytical reports that supported the development of Itaipuaçu’s masterplan. I conducted spatial analyses and territorial diagnostics using environmental, regulatory, and socioeconomic data, helping to identify local challenges and opportunities and providing evidence to inform urban planning decisions.
