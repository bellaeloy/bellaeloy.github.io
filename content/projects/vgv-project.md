### Description

VGV Optimization is a personal research project that explores how computational methods can **support real estate feasibility studies and urban design decision-making**. The project investigates how building massing, floor distribution, and the balance between covered and uncovered areas can be optimized to maximize the Gross Sales Value (GSV/VGV) while complying with planning regulations, including buildable envelopes, progressive setbacks, and development potential. Through the conceptual design of an optimization algorithm, the study translates urban planning constraints into computational parameters, creating a framework for evaluating alternative building configurations and supporting data-driven design decisions.

The project bridges urban planning, algorithmic thinking, and computational optimization, demonstrating how planning regulations can be transformed into programmable rules to support feasibility analysis and parametric urban design.
