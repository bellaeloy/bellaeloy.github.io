## Description

The International Hub for Sustainable Development (HIDS) is a planned smart innovation district in Campinas, Brazil, dedicated to developing solutions for sustainable development challenges. Covering 11.3 million square meters, it brings together leading academic, research, and technology institutions, including UNICAMP, PUC-Campinas, and the Ciatec High Technology Park. The project integrates research, innovation, urban planning, and environmental sustainability to create a model district that promotes collaboration among universities, government, industry, and society, supporting evidence-based strategies for resilient and sustainable urban development.

## My role

As part of the HIDS initiative, I contributed to sustainability and urban development studies through geospatial and territorial analyses. Working alongside researchers, planners, and public-sector stakeholders, I helped facilitate collaborative workshops and planning activities. My role also involved producing maps, spatial datasets, and technical reports that provided evidence to support strategic decisions and long-term development planning.
