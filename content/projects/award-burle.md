### Description

Received 3rd Place in the ABAP Roberto Burle Marx Award – Landscape Planning Practice Category, promoted by the Brazilian Association of Landscape Architects (ABAP). The award recognized the project “Landscape Connectivity: Planning the Multifunctional Network in Itaipuaçu (Maricá-RJ)”, which proposed a landscape planning strategy based on the integration of environmental systems, urban infrastructure, and community needs. The project developed a multifunctional network approach to enhance ecological connectivity, urban resilience, and the relationship between natural landscapes and everyday urban life.
