## Description

Co-authored with Marcelo Tramontano, the paper “Mapping Power and Collaboration in Urban Data Governance: A Complex Network Analysis of Social Actors in Brazil” was presented at the LASDIGOV, held at Gramado, RS. This paper investigates how power, collaboration, and knowledge exchange are structured within Brazil’s urban data governance ecosystem. Using Social Network Analysis (SNA) and critical data studies, the research maps relationships among civil society organizations, government institutions, academia, and funding agencies to reveal the sociotechnical networks shaping urban data governance. The study identifies key intermediary actors, regional disparities, and governance asymmetries, while introducing a replicable framework for analyzing collaborative networks and their influence on urban policymaking and data governance in the Global South.
