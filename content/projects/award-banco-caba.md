### Description

Co-designed with Luiz Fernando Albuquerque de Moraes (Nando Albuquerque), the Cabaça Bench is a sustainable urban furniture project developed at the Federal University of Pernambuco (UFPE). The project explores the reuse of plastic waste as a material for innovative design solutions, transforming recycled plastic sheets into a functional and environmentally conscious piece of urban furniture. The bench was produced at the Precious Plastic UFPE laboratory in collaboration with the GREA 3D group and supported by students throughout the fabrication process. The project was selected for official exhibition as part of the UFPE’s museum and institutional collection, highlighting the potential of circular design, digital fabrication, and material experimentation in contemporary design practices.
