## Description

Co-authored with PhD. Leticia Teixeira Mendes, the conference paper “Form and Urban Life in Christopher Alexander’s Work: Translation of Patterns for Parametric Code” was presented at SIGraDi 2020. The study investigates the potential of parametric design beyond form generation, exploring its role as a bridge between theoretical frameworks and design practice. Based on Christopher Alexander’s theories of patterns and the relationship between form and urban life, the research proposes the translation of qualitative design principles into computational instruments capable of supporting decision-making processes in complex urban environments. The work contributes to discussions on computational design, digital methodologies, and the integration of urban theory, design education, and parametric modeling.
