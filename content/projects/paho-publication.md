## Description

The booklet *Health Promotion and Cities* is part of a publication series commemorating the 15th anniversary of Brazil’s National Health Promotion Policy (PNPS). Designed for health managers and professionals, it highlights practical connections between the PNPS and other public policies, programs, and initiatives. The publication explores urban life through the lenses of health promotion, social determinants of health, and equity. Grounded in an expanded concept of health, the PNPS promotes intersectoral collaboration, social participation, and integrated health networks to improve living conditions, reduce vulnerabilities, and advance health equity across territories.

## My role

I led the graphic design and visual production of the book *Health Promotion and Cities* (2024). I was responsible for developing the editorial layout, visual content, and communication materials, helping to make academic and technical knowledge more accessible, engaging, and widely disseminated to diverse audiences.
