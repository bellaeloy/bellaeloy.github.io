State housing programs deliver a finished house of 42 m². Families then extend
it themselves, and the extension often blocks the only window of the bedroom.

## The idea

Design for the extension from the start. Each of the twelve plans has:

- A stage-one core of 42 m² that meets the program budget.
- A marked growth zone of up to 28 m² on one side.
- A foundation and a roof that already reach into the growth zone.

The window of every stage-one room faces away from the growth zone. The family
can build stage two without a redesign, and without loss of light.

## The twelve plans

Four lot widths (5 m, 6.25 m, 7.5 m, 10 m) meet three orientations. Every plan
uses the same wall module of 1.25 m, so one block size serves the whole
catalogue.
