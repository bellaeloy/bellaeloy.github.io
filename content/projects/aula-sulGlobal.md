### Description

Organized and facilitated a three-session study group exploring the concept of the **Global South** through a decolonial lens, examining its historical development, geopolitical contradictions, and implications for architecture, urbanism, and research in Brazil. The program combined guided discussions of foundational decolonial and critical geography literature with collaborative mapping activities in Miro, where participants critically examined country classifications, geopolitical tensions, and decolonial urban perspectives. As organizer and facilitator, I designed the study program, curated the reading materials, coordinated the sessions, and led discussions that connected theoretical debates to contemporary urban research and collaborative knowledge production.
