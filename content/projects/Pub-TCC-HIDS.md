## Description

In this research I explored the application of Form-Based Codes (FBC) to promote more sustainable, people-centered, and equitable urban development in Brazil. Focusing on the International Hub for Sustainable Development (HIDS) in Campinas, the study examined how urban regulations can improve environmental quality and access to public open spaces. The project included a review of international FBC practices, an analysis of Brazilian planning regulations, and the development of a computational framework for implementing Privately Owned Public Spaces (POPS) within the HIDS masterplan.
