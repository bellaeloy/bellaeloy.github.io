### Description

Designed and delivered a three-day hybrid workshop for researchers from the Observatory of the Metropolises, introducing the use of Rhinoceros 3D and Grasshopper for parametric urban modeling. The course focused on applying computational design methods to the analysis and development of urban interventions in informal settlements (favelas), combining theoretical discussions with hands-on exercises. As both organizer and instructor, I developed the course structure, teaching materials, and practical activities, enabling participants to explore data-driven and parametric approaches to urban research and planning.
