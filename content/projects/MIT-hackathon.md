### Description

AmbientALL: a project created during the MIT Policy Hackathon 2020 focused on assessing cumulative environmental impacts on Environmental Justice (EJ) populations in Massachusetts. The project proposed an integrated framework to identify and map socio-environmental vulnerabilities by combining indicators related to pollution exposure, health impacts, climate risks, and proximity to hazardous sites. The solution included the development of a territorial scoring system, interactive mapping tools, recommendations for annual data updates, and community engagement strategies designed to support more equitable and evidence-based environmental decision-making.

### My Role

I was developer, researcher, and data visualization specialist, contributing to the project's methodological design, data integration and analysis, indicator development, and visual communication strategy. Responsible for territorial scoring frameworks, and decision-support tools for policymakers, researchers, and affected communities.
