### Description

The GHZ Network (Social Agents for Collective Intelligence and Urban Data Governance) was developed as part of a PhD research project investigating urban governance in Brazil. Conceived as a sociotechnical experiment, the initiative focused on modeling and visualizing the relationships among organizations, institutions, and social actors engaged in urban data governance. By integrating actors, data, and interactions within a shared informational environment, the project aimed to support collective intelligence processes and contribute to a deeper understanding of Brazil’s urban-digital ecosystem and governance dynamics.

### My Role

As part of my doctoral research, I designed and developed the GHZ Network as an experimental platform for mapping and analyzing relationships among actors involved in urban data governance. I collected, structured, and analyzed data on organizations and their connections, producing network visualizations and analytical outputs that revealed collaboration patterns, institutional linkages, and governance structures. This work helped create new ways of understanding urban governance through data-driven and visual approaches.
