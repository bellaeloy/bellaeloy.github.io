### Description

As a Teaching Assistant at the University of São Paulo (USP), I designed and delivered a short course on **Geoprocessing and QGIS** for third-year undergraduate Architecture and Urbanism students. The course consisted of **three 4-hour sessions** and was developed to support the students' work in the **Project VII** studio. The course introduced the fundamentals of QGIS, including spatial data management, map creation, and geospatial analysis. It also covered collaborative data management strategies, file organization, and workflows for team-based projects, along with best practices for integrating geospatial information into the urban design process.

The course was delivered under the supervision of Professors Marcelo Tramontano and Jeferson Tavares.
