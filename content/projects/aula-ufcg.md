### Description

Invited by the Architecture and Urbanism program at the Federal University of Campina Grande (UFCG), I designed and delivered a 20-hour short course on Introduction to Parametric Technology. The course brought together approximately 20 undergraduate and graduate students, introducing them to the fundamentals of computational and parametric design. Through hands-on activities, participants explored algorithmic thinking and digital tools for architectural design, digital fabrication, and computational problem-solving.
