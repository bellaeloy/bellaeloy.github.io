Hi, I'm Isabella Cavalcanti. I'm an architect and urban designer. I hold a master's degree
in urban development, and I work with parametric modeling.

Two questions hold my work together. What shape does a city take? And which
rules produce that shape? A zoning table, a subsidy and a bus route each leave a
mark on the ground. I like to make that mark visible before anyone pours
concrete.

That is why I model. A Grasshopper definition lets me test twenty versions of a
block in an afternoon. A QGIS layer lets me ask whether the twenty versions
change anyone's walk to school. The drawing then argues from evidence, not from
taste alone.

I also write code for the data side of this work. Python and open public data
turn a claim about a city into a map that a council member can read in a
meeting. Some of that work is on [GitHub](https://github.com/bellaeloy).

Outside of that, I care about public space, about housing that families can
extend, and about groups that govern their own data.

Get in touch if you work on any of those.
