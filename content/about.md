### Hello!

I’m Isabella Cavalcanti, an urban data analyst, designer and researcher passionate about understanding cities through data, technology, and design.

My work sits at the intersection of urban planning, data science, and digital innovation, exploring how information, geospatial technologies, and collaborative approaches can help create better cities and more effective public policies. I have experience working with data analysis, GIS, spatial analysis, indicator development, and the organization of complex datasets to support decision-making in urban and social projects.

Throughout my academic and professional journey, I have collaborated with universities, public institutions, and civil society organizations, combining research, programming, and visual communication to transform data into meaningful insights.

I am always interested in connecting with people and organizations working on urban challenges, data-driven solutions, public policy, and digital transformation. If you have a project, research opportunity, collaboration, or simply want to exchange ideas, feel free to get in touch!
